An image is a cache with a name on top

Every Dockerfile instruction becomes one layer, and every layer's id is a hash of its parent, the instruction, and the files it touched. Change any of those and that layer, plus everything above it, gets rebuilt. Edit, build, and watch where the cache breaks.
