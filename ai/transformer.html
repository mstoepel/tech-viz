Attention is a weighted lookup over everything that came before

Every token asks every earlier token one question, turns the answers into weights that sum to one, and mixes their vectors in those proportions. Do that with a few dozen "heads" per layer, stack a few layers, and you have a transformer. Pick a token, a layer, and a head, and watch the numbers.

Engine notes. Same shell as the other sandboxes, plus clickable controls that issue the same commands. The arithmetic is real: token and position embeddings, a score per (query, key) pair, a causal mask, a softmax with temperature, a weighted sum of value vectors added into a residual stream, an MLP step, and a KV cache with operation counts. What is not real: the scores come from hand-written functions that imitate head types found in trained models (previous-token, sink, local, duplicate, induction, pronoun-to-noun) rather than from learned query/key projections. Tokens are words here; real models use subword pieces.
